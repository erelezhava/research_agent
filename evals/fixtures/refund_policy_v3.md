# Customer Refund Policy — version 3.0 (synthetic evaluation fixture)

Effective date: 1 March 2026. Owner: Finance Operations.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to refunds for card and bank-transfer payments made through the online store.
Marketplace (third-party seller) orders are out of scope and follow the seller's own policy.

## 2. Approval thresholds
Refunds of up to 500 GEL may be approved by a Customer Support Team Lead.
Refunds above 500 GEL require approval by the Finance Manager within 2 business days of the request.
Refunds above 5,000 GEL additionally require sign-off by the Chief Financial Officer.

## 3. Time limits
Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery.
Requests received after 30 days are rejected unless the product is defective under warranty.

## 4. Payment method
Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Cash refunds are not permitted.
